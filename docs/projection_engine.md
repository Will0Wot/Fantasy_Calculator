# Projection engine

A game-by-game Monte Carlo for fantasy matchups. Code: `tradecalc/sim/`. Fitted parameters:
`tradecalc/config/sim_params.yaml`, regenerated by `tradecalc sim-fit`.

```bash
tradecalc sim-fit                          # fit everything on the two latest seasons (~30 s)
tradecalc simulate --a 1 --b 4 --week 3    # any two fantasy teams, any week
tradecalc sim-validate                     # out-of-sample check on last season
```

The app's **Simulate** tab runs the same thing, with score distributions and per-player stat lines.

## What happens in one simulated week

For every NBA game in the week, and for each of 10,000 draws:

1. **Who plays.** Each rotation player on both NBA teams gets a probability of playing:
   - The injury model (return dates, injury report status, injury history) gives his availability that day.
   - The rest model adjusts it for back-to-backs.

   Absences are drawn jointly, so a draw where the star sits is a draw where his teammates absorb his role.
2. **Game shocks shared by everyone in the game.**
   - A final margin, drawn from team strength, home court, and a ±14-point spread. Blowouts cut starters' minutes and add bench minutes.
   - A pace shock of about ±4%.
3. **Minutes.**
   - Expected minutes, plus a share of absent teammates' minutes (same position counts double), plus the blowout effect, plus noise.
   - The team's minutes are then scaled so they add up to a real game's total.
4. **Usage.** Shot, free-throw, assist and turnover rates rise with the share of team usage that is absent.
5. **Opponent.** Per-stat defensive factors, pace, and absent opposing defenders adjust each rate.
6. **Box score.** Built from shots upward, so every line is internally consistent:
   - FGA ~ Poisson. 3PA, 2PM and 3PM ~ Binomial.
   - FTA ~ negative binomial, then FTM ~ Binomial.
   - REB, AST, STL and BLK ~ negative binomial. TOV ~ Poisson.
   - Points = 2 × 2PM + 3 × 3PM + FTM.
7. **Form.** One multiplier per player per simulated week (±18%) correlates his games within the week.

Then each fantasy day, the manager starts the best eligible lineup among players who are active. Starters' points are summed, and the two fantasy teams are compared.

## Dates and the current week

- **Today's date** is shown everywhere: the app banner and the first line of every CLI command. Before opening night, projections start from opening night.
- **Only the days left in the week are simulated.** For days that have already happened, each team's actual fantasy points from ESPN are added to its total. A Thursday run is "Mon–Wed actual + Thu–Sun simulated".
- **The app shows the weekly schedule as a grid:** ● for a game still to play, ○ for one already played. "Games left" counts only remaining games.
- **To see the week from another day,** set "Pretend today is" in the sidebar, or pass `--today YYYY-MM-DD` on the command line.

## Components and what the data said

| Component | How it's estimated | Notable finding |
|---|---|---|
| Per-stat rates (FGA, FTA, 3PA share, 2P%, 3P%, FT%, REB, AST, STL, BLK, TOV per minute) | Empirical Bayes: this season shrunk toward last season, which is shrunk toward the position average. Shrinkage per stat is chosen by predicting even-numbered games from odd-numbered games. | Shot attempts, rebounds and assists are trustworthy after 25–50 minutes; steals need about 250 minutes and 3P% about 300 attempts. |
| Minutes | Recent-game average plus redistribution from absent teammates, back-to-back effect, and blowouts. Preseason minutes blend last season, ESPN depth-chart slot, and ESPN projected minutes. | A player 30+ minutes deep loses about 0.3 min per point of margin beyond 12; absent minutes flow about twice as much to the same position. |
| Usage | Per-minute rate / baseline − 1, regressed on the missing share of team usage | With 20% of team shots absent: FGA +7%, FTA +9%, AST +6.5%, REB +1.4% |
| Team defense | Opponent per-possession stats allowed, shrunk per stat | 3P% allowed is almost pure noise; steals, blocks and turnovers forced vary ±20%, free throws allowed ±10% |
| Defender availability | Last season's on/off defensive rating, heavily shrunk | Small: a few points per 100 possessions at most |
| Rest on back-to-backs | Logistic regression on single-game absences of healthy rotation players | Sitting rate doubles on back-to-backs (9.4% vs 4.7%) and reaches 17% for players 34+ |
| Coach tendencies | Per-coach rates, shrunk by how persistent they proved | Rotation shape persists (r ≈ 0.7) and is used when a team changes coach; rest habits barely persist (r ≈ 0.1) and are shrunk hard |
| Injury report | Status → play probability via the injury model (`docs/injury_research.md`) | ESPN gives "day-to-day" without probable/questionable/doubtful detail; see Limits |

## Validation (fit on 2023-24 + 2024-25, tested on 2025-26)

**Point projections per game** (about 3,950 player-games, each predicted only from earlier data):

| | MAE | RMSE |
|---|---|---|
| Trade calculator's FPPG (season FPPG shrunk to last season) | 10.05 | 12.94 |
| Engine | **9.68** | **12.25** |
| Engine, not told who is out | 9.77 | 12.56 |
| Engine given actual minutes | 7.66 | 10.06 |

The last row shows most remaining error is minutes, which are inherently noisy in a single game. That noise is why the simulator draws minutes rather than using an average.

**Calibration of simulated ranges** (5 test weeks; player availability fixed to what happened, to isolate minutes and stat randomness):

| | Target | Single games | Player-weeks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inside 80% range | 80% | 79.5% | 79.7% |
| Inside 50% range | 50% | 50.9% | 51.6% |
| Bias (FP) | 0 | −0.01 | −0.04 |

**Fantasy matchups.** Random 13-man rosters on real 2025-26 weeks, comparing predicted win chance to the actual result:
- Brier score 0.18–0.22 across two runs, against 0.25 for a coin flip.
- The middle of the range is well calibrated.
- The extremes are slightly overconfident in backtests. Those backtests lack injury reports, so real weeks had more unexpected absences than the simulator assumed.

## Limits

- **Lineups are modeled at the game level, not the stint level.** The model knows which teammates are active, not which five share the floor. Stint data would need play-by-play.
- **No probable/questionable/doubtful detail.** ESPN reports only "day-to-day" (treated as 50%). Each refresh snapshots every player's status per day (`status_snapshots` table), so the real play rate can be measured once the season has data. The fitting step for that isn't built yet.
- **Team strength (net rating) uses last season until about 300 games are played,** so offseason roster changes aren't reflected in margins and blowouts early on.
- **Coach effects are inferred from team-seasons.** Mid-season coaching changes aren't detected.
